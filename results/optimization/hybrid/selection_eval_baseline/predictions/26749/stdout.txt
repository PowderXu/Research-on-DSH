{"answer":"Cause: secrets are not automatically available to reusable workflows — the called workflow must declare allowed secrets under on.workflow_call.secrets and the caller must pass matching names via jobs.<job_id>.secrets (or use secrets: inherit). Your error means the caller passed a secret named github-token that the reusable workflow did not declare. Fix: either add the secret declaration to the reusable workflow (on.workflow_call.secrets: github-token: {}) or pass the secret from the caller with the same name, e.g. jobs.<id>: uses: owner/repo/.github/workflows/auto-merge.yml@ref secrets: github-token: ${{ secrets.GH_TOKEN }} (or use secrets: inherit).","sources":["actions/how-tos/reuse-automations/reuse-workflows.md","actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/workflow-syntax.md","actions/how-tos/write-workflows/choose-what-workflows-do/use-secrets.md"]}
